# Concept library

Nine starting points, each built on a structure that worked in a reference film and written for Clipzing's real
features. Pitch three (judged against the brief), pick one, then make it NEW: keep the device, invent the moves.
Every line here passes `qa.claims_lint`. Re-check against product-facts.md anyway.

Short = 9:16 first, 15–25 s + a 2 s end card. Long = 16:9 first, 45–120 s. Both get a real re-layout for the other ratio.

---
## 1. The Path: one line of light, many jobs
**Device:** one purple line of light does every job. **Structure:** a camera flight between stations.
**Works for:** brand awareness, the whole product in one breath.
**VO (one flowing read):** "One episode, that's all it takes. One clip at a time, a streak you'll want to keep
alive, a chart you'll want to top, and then, one day... they find you. Shorts, TikTok, Reels... all from one
upload. One episode, all your shorts. Clipzing."
| s | Station |
|---|---|
| 0.0–1.7 | Zing slams at 0.17 s (half the frame), shards, shake; the line runs under "Clipzing" and reveals it; blink, hop |
| 1.7–2.9 | The line dives along an S-curve of chunky chapter nodes (a two-hour episode); presses GENERATE CLIPS; a phone rises |
| 2.9–4.8 | The clip: caption tiles snap in on the words; the score ring fills to 94; the `clip_ready` chime; the bar fills with the line |
| 4.8–7.2 | The line curls into a streak flame (the one anamorphic streak); the counter 1 → 365 in fixed cells; the week row fills |
| 7.2–9.3 | Drop: "Your top clips this week"; your row climbs #7 → #1; the chime again (after the word) |
| 9.3–11.4 | A floating phone plays Zing's podcast; the line becomes its voice wave; comments and followers arrive |
| 11.4–14.7 | Pull-back: the line splits into the THREE real platforms (YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Reels), each lit on its word |
| 14.7–16.5 | Type: "One episode," / "all your shorts." with the line as the baseline |
| 16.5–21 | A breath; Zing lands ON the hit; the line reveals the wordmark; `published`; the end card |
**Music:** `mallet_pop` 100 BPM D major; drop on "chart"; lift on the fork; breath + hit on the landing.
**Long version (60 s):** give each station its feature line (detection, captions, calendar, autopilot) and a 2 s hold.
**Lesson:** the first version said "even LinkedIn". Clipzing can't post there. Platforms come from product-facts.md.

## 2. Word Slam → The Link (a prompt's journey)
**Device:** words slam on the frame they're spoken, then ONE link travels through the product.
**Works for:** podcasters and YouTubers; a direct-response hook; the clearest "what it does".
**VO:** "Today, your podcast gets shorter. Paste one link. It finds the moments worth posting, frames the
speaker, and times every word. Ranked. Captioned. Ready. Clipzing."
| s | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0.0–2.4 | WORD SLAM on the words: TODAY, / YOUR PODCAST / GETS / SHORTER. Each rushes out of depth, lands full-width, a decaying shake, a sub boom. SHORTER. is the biggest |
| 2.4–3.4 | The dark frame collapses into the "Generate clips" input; a YouTube link types in letter by letter (a tick per letter) |
| 3.4–5.0 | Tap (press into the lip, click); the link becomes the line; it runs into a long waveform of the episode |
| 5.0–7.2 | Scan light; moments lift out of the waveform as cards; score rings fill and the cards SORT best-first |
| 7.2–9.0 | Drop: a 16:9 frame of Zing's podcast; a 9:16 window glides with the speaker ("frames the speaker") |
| 9.0–11.0 | Real Clipzing captions type on, word by word, in the clip ("times every word") |
| 11.0–13.5 | Phones rise into a row: three clips with score badges; "Ranked. Captioned. Ready." as three slams |
| 13.5–16 | Light gathers into Zing's icon → the wordmark; `published`; end card |
**Music:** `keynote` (tense under the slams, drop on "link", the biggest drop on "Ranked").

## 3. Found (a raid, but for a small creator)
**Device:** viewers are particles of light. **Structure:** many → one → found. Aspirational, a story rather than a promise.
**VO:** "Right now, thousands of shorts are going up. And somewhere, a great episode is sitting at three views.
Clip it. Caption it. Send it out. Give your best moment a chance. Clipzing."
| s | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0.0–0.8 | Countdown 3 / 2 / 1 punching the glass on 8th notes, then an inhale |
| 0.8–1.7 | SLAM: a swarm of light erupts; warp lines; push into the light |
| 1.7–4.2 | Iris match-cut: the light shrinks into the first short card on a stage; cards pop 1 → 3×3 → 7×7 → a wall, on the beats |
| 4.2–7.6 | Every card falls away in a wave toward ONE: Zing's podcast, 3 viewers; three lights orbit it and merge. Dead stop |
| 7.6–11.7 | The drop: Clipzing's light hits it; caption words, a reframe, a score ring; the 3 rolls to 12,483 in fixed cells; comments spiral |
| 11.7–14 | "Give your best moment a chance." rises; the type dissolves into the swarm |
| 14–16 | Every particle lands into Zing (`swarm` → cross-fade to the sprite on the landing frame) → the wordmark |
**Music:** `future_bass` 144 BPM; a TRUE dead stop before the drop (`silence=` in the mix). **Care:** numbers are a
story. Never caption them as a result Clipzing guarantees.

## 4. Just Ask (a feature launch inside a known product)
**Device:** the product you know, then "and now…" three real examples, a drop on the logo, "Now with ___".
**Works for:** launching the copilot, AI agents (MCP), Autopilot, or any new feature.
**VO:** "Your whole show, in one place. Clip, caption, and schedule with Clipzing. And now, you can just ask.
'Give these three clips punchier titles.' 'Schedule my five best clips across next week.' It always asks before
anything goes live. Clipzing. Now with copilot."
| s | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0.0–1.1 | HOOK: Zing's icon rushes from depth (never tumbling) and slams dead-centre at 0.27 s: a camera punch, a micro shake, a light band across it; the camera flies through it |
| 1.1–3.2 | The dashboard (real captured screen) on a big plane; "My Clips" grid |
| 3.2–5.3 | Clip / caption / schedule: three fast UI moments, one per spoken verb |
| 5.3–6.5 | "And now," the copilot spark blooms out of the corner exactly on "ask"; the dashboard dims |
| 6.5–10 | The chat: the user's line types in; three clip titles rewrite themselves (a tick per letter) |
| 10–12.5 | "Schedule my five best…": five clips fly onto calendar days, YouTube "Scheduled" |
| 12.5–14 | The approval card: "Publish 5 posts?" Confirm pressed on "asks"; a progress ring closes; ✓ |
| 14–16 | DROP on the downbeat: the wordmark, centred, held. "Now with copilot." ("copilot" in the accent) |
**Music:** `keynote` 112.5 BPM; a breath + bell when the copilot answers; a riser into the drop.
**Swap-ins:** agents ("Use Clipzing from Claude or ChatGPT"); Autopilot ("And now, it watches your channel").

## 5. Already Clipped (Autopilot, one continuous light)
**Device:** one light travels from your upload to your calendar. "See what's next" for creators.
**VO:** "You upload on Tuesday. Clipzing is already on it. The moments, found. The speaker, framed. Every word,
timed. On your calendar before you've planned the next episode. Review mode holds every clip for your OK.
Autopilot, by Clipzing."
Stations: a YouTube-style upload card (fictional channel) → light drops into the Autopilot settings card ("Review
mode: on") → moments → reframe → captions → a calendar filling across the week (YouTube "Scheduled", TikTok "Sent to
drafts") → the review queue with an approve button → the wordmark.
**Music:** `keynote` or `mallet_pop`. **Facts:** Creator 1 channel, Agency 5; new uploads only.

## 6. Two Hours In, Shorts Out (before → after)
**Device:** a split-world before/after with Zing as the creator. Before: `tense` Zing scrubbing an endless timeline
(dim, slow, ticking clock). After: one link, shorts out (bright, on the beat).
**VO:** "Two hours of podcast. Hours of scrubbing for one good minute. Or... paste the link. Clipzing finds the
moments, frames the speaker, and times every word. Two hours in. Shorts out. Clipzing."
Camera: the timeline stretches to infinity (depth flight), then on "Or..." a dead stop, Zing turns `determined`,
the link input slams in, and the world re-lights purple. The payoff is a fan of clips with score badges.
**Music:** `cinematic` (D minor) for the before, then the drop flips to `mallet_pop` D major on "paste".

## 7. Pick a Look (the real caption engine, as a runway)
**Device:** the same sentence re-styled by Clipzing's REAL caption templates on every beat (capture/src/captions.tsx).
**VO:** "Every word, timed. Every style, yours. Captions in more than thirty languages, matched to your brand.
Clipzing captions."
Each phrase renders in a different real template (e.g. Ivo, Pepper, Selene, Sol, Nyx, Kai) inside Zing's podcast
footage, switching ON the beat. The brand-kit beat recolours one into Clipzing purple. The end card lands on the wordmark.
**Facts:** don't state a style count (38 in code vs 10/15 on the features page). Brand-matched captions are a
Creator/Agency feature.

## 8. Clipzing in 90 Seconds (long-form product film, 16:9)
**Device:** a guided tour through ONE continuous dashboard world, Zing presenting beside each station.
**Chapters (each 8–12 s, with a super and one wow):**
1. Hook (0–6 s): word slam "TWO HOURS IN. / SHORTS OUT." → the wordmark.
2. Link in (Generate clips, YouTube link or upload, up to two hours).
3. The moments (detection, score 0–100, best first, title/description/hashtags written).
4. The frame (auto reframe by genre: podcast, lesson, stream, sport; a 9:16 window gliding over 16:9).
5. The words (real captions, 30+ languages, fix a misheard word in the editor).
6. The edit (edit by the words; B-roll and a hook voiceover on Creator and up; only Export renders).
7. The post (one calendar; YouTube Shorts published; TikTok to drafts; Reels-ready export).
8. Autopilot (new uploads become clips; review mode).
9. Ask (copilot + Claude/ChatGPT via the connector; approvals).
10. Plans (optional: "Plans from $14.99 a month. Cancel anytime." Re-check the price that day) and the end card.
**VO:** a calm, warm narrator, one flowing take (90 s ≈ 210–230 words). **Music:** `keynote`, with a lift at each
chapter, sections at 30 fps if the render budget needs it.
**9:16 cut:** chapters 1, 2, 3, 5, 7 + end (≈ 35 s).

## 9. One Feature, One Film (a series template)
For each feature in product-facts.md: Hook (the pain in one line, 1.5 s) → the three "how" steps from
features.ts as three stations → the honest limit, framed positively (e.g. "TikTok gets a draft, so the last touch is
yours") → the end card. 20–30 s, 9:16 + 16:9. Good for a weekly posting cadence.

## Writing a NEW concept
1. One sentence: "One ___, many jobs." (or "many → one", "before → after", "and now…").
2. The hook is a slam you can read on mute within 0.3 s, with the brand inside 2 s.
3. Three to six stations, each a real feature with ONE true line and ONE picture that proves it.
4. A hero moment (Zing lands / the logo drops) with silence or a breath before it.
5. Lint the script. Read it aloud at natural speed: it sets the length, not the other way round.
