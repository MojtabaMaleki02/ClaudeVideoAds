# Got You: brief

## The ask (owner, 2026-10-19)
A promotional video with a story: open on "your new audience is scrolling short feeds; stuck at 200 followers? don't
worry, I got you", then introduce: a long-video URL → multiple highlight shorts; multiple high-quality captions;
smart reframing (streaming, podcasts, gaming); B-roll; scheduling across the week ("never miss a day"); the
official icons of Instagram, YouTube and TikTok.

## Story
A creator is stuck at 200 followers while their next followers scroll short videos. Zing slams in: "Don't worry. I
got you." Then one feature per beat, each with a picture that proves it, ending on "Clipzing. Long videos in.
Shorts out."

## Truth adjustments (product-facts.md)
- "Most of your new audience…" became "your next followers are scrolling short videos right now". There's no source
  for "most".
- Scheduling: YouTube Shorts are scheduled and published; TikTok receives drafts (they can't be scheduled for later);
  Instagram gets a Reels-ready export (no posting). The calendar shows exactly that ("6 pm" / "Draft" / "Reels"), and
  the legend under the official marks says "Shorts · published", "TikTok · to drafts", "Reels · export".
- The official marks come from simple-icons (CC0 shapes, as on Clipzing's own site), in each brand's colours, used
  only to show where Clipzing's clips go.
- Game footage is an original neon shooter drawn in code (no real game or IP). Every phone shows Zing's own podcast.

## Script (91 words, one flowing read, claims_lint clean)
"Did you know your next followers are scrolling short videos right now? Stuck at two hundred followers, with no
idea how to grow? Don't worry. I got you. Paste a YouTube link, and Clipzing turns your long video into short clips
of the best moments. Captions that pop, in the style you like, timed to every word. Podcast, stream, or gameplay, it
keeps the right thing in frame. Add B-roll where it helps. Then schedule your clips across the week, and never miss
a day. Clipzing. Long videos in. Shorts out."
